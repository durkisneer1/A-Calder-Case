# A Calder Case

When Nola Vega is found dead, the city turns quiet ...but not clean.

Unravel a tangled web of lies, jealousy, and long-buried resentment as Detective Calder searching for the truth behind her murder. Investigate the crime scene, speak with persons of interest, and follow the threads before they disappear into the dark.

Use your wits to gather evidence and piece together what really happened.  

But be careful: the wrong question can close doors forever.
